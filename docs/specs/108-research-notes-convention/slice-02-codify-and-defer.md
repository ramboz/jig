## Slice 108-02 — codify in conventions.md + register deferred machinery

**Goal:** Turn the 108-01 convention into governed canon (a `docs/conventions.md`
entry) and register ADR-0054's deferred machinery as trigger-gated
`docs/refinement-todo.md` entries, so the convention is discoverable in the
constitution and the un-built pieces have explicit resolution triggers.

**DoR:**
- ✅ Slice 108-01 DONE — the convention (template + index + hand-offs) exists to
  be codified and cited.
- ⛔ **Human approval to edit `docs/conventions.md`** — the repo constraint
  (CLAUDE.md: "Do not modify `docs/conventions.md` without explicit human
  approval") and the `jig-spec-gate` deliberateness gate require
  `JIG_CONVENTIONS_APPROVED=1`. This slice cannot reach
  `READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION` for the conventions.md AC until that approval is
  granted. (The refinement-todo AC below needs no such approval.)

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. **`docs/conventions.md` gains a research-notes entry** (with
   `JIG_CONVENTIONS_APPROVED=1`) that states, tersely: the home
   (`docs/research/R-NNN-<slug>.md`); the **phase distinction** — a research
   note is an *open investigation with no decision named yet*, sequential with
   (not competing against) a `refinement-todo` *named deferred decision +
   trigger*; the two hand-offs; and that numbering is local-and-cheap, not
   reservation-coordinated. Links to [ADR-0054](../../decisions/adr-0054-research-notes-artifact-convention.md).
2. **`docs/refinement-todo.md` registers the deferred machinery**, each as a
   `### Decision:` entry with an explicit **Resolution trigger** (ADR-0054's
   deferrals): (a) `research.py` creation helper; (b) index regeneration; (c)
   link-resolution linter; (d) numbering-collision handling; (e) `scaffold-init`
   adopter-facing surface. Triggers follow jig's demand-gating idiom (e.g. "Nth
   note created and hand-index drift observed", "a concurrent double-`R-NNN`
   collision actually bites", "an adopter asks for research notes").
3. **ADR-0054's open questions are dispositioned.** The `conventions.md` entry
   resolves the "codify in conventions.md" open question; the adopter-default
   open question is recorded as deferred entry (e) with its trigger. The
   distinctness kill-criterion is referenced as the tripwire, not re-litigated.
