## Slice 02 — grounding-requirement

**Goal:** Spec/ADR authoring requires factual claims about runnable surfaces to
be probe-backed (or marked as assumptions), and surfaces the resulting
load-bearing assumptions in a first-class section — so the very act of authoring
produces the signal slice 04 triggers on.

**DoR:**
- ✅ 064-01 returned **go**.
- ✅ Templates located: `templates/docs/decisions/adr-0000-template.md`,
      `templates/docs/specs/slice-template.md`, spec.md shape.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. **The spec/ADR templates carry an `## Assumptions` (and, for ADRs, a
   `## Kill criteria`) section** with guidance that load-bearing factual claims
   about runnable surfaces (library/API capability, version behavior, perf,
   existing-code behavior) must be backed by an executed probe or citation, or
   explicitly listed here as an assumption. The section is *risk-gated* — guidance
   states it may be omitted when an artifact has no unverified load-bearing
   assumptions (do not mandate boilerplate).
2. **The author-step contract reflects the rule** — `agents/architect.md` (and/or
   the spec-author guidance the orchestrator follows) instructs: ground claims by
   probe first, mark the rest as assumptions, never assert. Observable as updated
   prose in the agent/skill contract file(s).
3. **A worked example demonstrates the standard** — at least one existing or new
   artifact shows a probe-grounded claim and an explicitly-marked assumption,
   provable by reading the file.
